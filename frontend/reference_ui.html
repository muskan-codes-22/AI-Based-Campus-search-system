What does the 2024 leave policy say about carrying forward unused leave?
Assistant

Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to 15 days into the next calendar year. Any balance above that limit lapses on 31 March unless the manager approves an exception in writing.

Carried-forward leave must be used by the end of the first quarter. It is not paid out on resignation.

Sources
  • leave_policy_2024.pdf p. 7
  • hr_faq_v3.docx section 4.2
Does this apply to contract staff as well?
Assistant

The policy covers full-time employees only. Contract staff are governed by their individual agreements, which are not part of the indexed collection.
